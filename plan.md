# BERAFLIX redesign and FrameX integration plan

## Product, scope, and constraints

Continue the existing mobile-first Next.js 15 App Router application branded **BERAFLIX — BY BERA TECH**. The approved visual reference is the user-supplied BERAFLIX screenshot. Its actual canvas is charcoal/black with white typography and vivid red accents; it is not a white-page theme. Preserve the existing BERAFLIX identity and working product behavior while replacing the current violet/blue emphasis with the reference's red/black language.

Keep the protected FrameX credential server-side, send only the authorized BERAFLIX origin in protected API requests, and preserve the existing canonical domain, managed runtime, device-local library, browser-managed download limitations, and already-enabled automatic publication. Do not use a proxy or VPN to evade geographic restrictions. Keep the supported movie catalog MovieBox-only; do not reintroduce Showbox. Reject unsupported or explicitly conflicting source records. Preserve the difference between a title lookup ID and its canonical content ID.

Integrate only publicly documented FrameX routes, with defensive response normalization. FrameX's public documentation lists current active routes but has no dated changelog, so describe these as documented/current features rather than claiming they are newly released. Use OmniSave only as inspiration from its visible search-first, quality/subtitle, and episode-queue workflow. Do not copy or reverse-engineer its private backend or undocumented routes. FrameX documents no music catalog or MP3 service. Leave music/MP3 out until an authorized, documented music provider is supplied.

The regular MovieBox series routes do not document a season/episode list endpoint. Preserve tap-to-select numeric season/episode controls and the existing one-at-a-time range queue; do not invent an episode count or label an unverified range as a whole season. Browser-managed transfer progress/completion remains outside the website's visibility; report only link preparation and browser handoff.

## Reference-led design direction

- **Design Movement:** Cinematic OTT editorial design, following the user's BERAFLIX screenshot's strong black canvas, white type, and bright-red wayfinding.
- **Core Principles:** Lead with the title and artwork; make navigation/search immediate; keep rows dense but readable; present only verified availability and status.
- **Color Philosophy:** Use `#08090D` and charcoal surfaces for depth, white for clear hierarchy, muted gray for supporting metadata, `#F01436` for primary crimson actions, and `#B50929` for secondary crimson details. Do not use yellow or gold. Keep red accents strategic, with restrained gradients and no oversized red surfaces.
- **Layout Paradigm:** On desktop, use a persistent left navigation rail beside a broad content column, a compact top bar with search and a profile-avatar action, and a large image-led feature banner followed by horizontal shelves such as Trending, Continue Watching, and Recommended. On phones, keep the same content hierarchy but use safe-area-aware fixed bottom navigation, full-width artwork, horizontal rails, and touch-sized controls. Preserve the five primary destinations: Discover, Explore, My Shelf, Downloads, and Profile; Movies, Series, Anime, Sports, Channels, Books, Wrestling, and Channels V2 remain browse modes inside Explore.
- **Signature Elements:** A red angular play/film mark; a narrow red active-navigation rail; vivid red primary buttons over layered charcoal surfaces.
- **Interaction Philosophy:** One tap to search, browse a format, open details, or choose an episode. Fetch secondary rows only when useful. Keep statuses honest and errors retryable. Retain keyboard access, visible focus, and reduced-motion support.
- **Animation:** No loading spinners, rotating icons, shimmer, pulsing skeletons, or animated loading text. If skeleton shapes are shown while data is pending, keep them static. Keep hover/focus transitions brief and restrained; honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Typography System:** Use the existing system sans-serif stack with bold, compact display headings, readable body copy, and small uppercase tracking only for metadata labels. Add no remote font dependency.
- **Brand Essence:** A direct, image-led discovery and personal-library app for BERA TECH viewers; **cinematic, fast, trustworthy**.
- **Brand Voice:** Direct and title-first. Examples: “Find your next watch.” “Choose an episode to continue.”
- **Wordmark & Logo:** Use a distinctive red play-chevron/film-frame symbol with a custom BERAFLIX wordmark lockup and “BY BERA TECH”; keep the existing brand name and do not copy third-party logos or artwork.
- **Signature Brand Color:** BERAFLIX crimson `#F01436`; use `#B50929` as the secondary crimson.

## Implementation approach

### Shell, Discover, and Explore

Restyle `app/globals.css` and `components/AppShell.js` around the screenshot's sidebar, compact search/profile header, active-red navigation, and phone bottom bar. Update the custom angular mark, crimson-highlighted wordmark, app icon, and durable `app.config.ts` logo URL consistently. Keep the existing large featured backdrop with title, actual metadata, Watch Now/Details/Watchlist actions, and carousel controls, followed by source-backed horizontal shelves. Use only returned title artwork and metadata; do not reproduce the screenshot's sample titles, fabricated data, or external brand elements.

Keep the server-rendered shell independent from slow upstream catalog calls. Add a Suspense boundary or equivalent streaming split so navigation, search, and stable page structure can appear before secondary catalog requests finish. Replace animated loading UI in `app/loading.js`, `components/PlayerScreen.js`, `components/SportsScreen.js`, and other affected components with concise static statuses or stable placeholders. Preserve meaningful error, retry, empty, playback, accessibility, reduced-motion, and navigation behavior.

Add server-only catalog helpers in `lib/catalog.js` for the documented `/v1/categories`, `/v1/genres`, `/v1/movies/upcoming`, and relevant MovieBox series endpoints. Expose same-origin handlers only where client interaction needs them. Integrate category/genre filters into Explore and add a source-backed Upcoming shelf. A live query found 11 labels in each metadata route, but all 10 non-Upcoming slugs in each family currently return empty `results`; the Upcoming route returns 20 titles. Keep filters selectable with truthful empty states and do not imply that those titles are available until FrameX returns them. Avoid making the first paint depend on these secondary endpoints. Keep ordinary catalog metadata cached with a bounded revalidation interval; do not cache signed playback, embed, caption, or download URLs.

Keep the legacy Channels schedule mode inside Explore. Load the public directory from `GET /v1/channels` and programme lists from `GET /v1/channels/:channelId/schedule` through same-origin server handlers. The live directory listed IDs 14, 15, 19, and 31; the user-supplied ID 9 schedule returned records but ID 9 was not in the directory, so label it only as “Channel 9” and do not invent channel metadata. The default schedule response is usable; the documentation does not define the optional `day` format, and a date-formatted probe returned an empty list, so do not add a day selector yet. Display source-provided program names, status, and time strings as returned, with explicit empty/error states. Add Channels V2 as a separate country/channel catalogue. The live probe and a 512-byte manifest request verified the exact HTTPS FrameX HLS relay path and documented 10-minute token lifetime; expose playback only through a user-triggered, same-origin no-store resolver, refresh before expiry, and never expose raw provider stream arrays.

Improve search so successful supported FrameX search responses—including valid empty results—return without waiting for a full five-route catalog fetch. Use the cached catalog only when the search request fails, normalize and de-duplicate by the signed detail path, and retain the current query/mode behavior. Add Explore sorting by title, release year, or returned rating only; do not infer values that are not supplied.

### Movies, series, Anime, Sports, captions, and download workflow

Keep MovieBox Movie/Series data and media calls behind Next.js server code with the protected key. Use canonical IDs for detail, stream, attachment, category, and caption requests. Preserve current signed-link expiry validation, media Range requests, player quality choices, resume state, brightness/volume/fullscreen behavior, region-availability messages, and download-link restrictions. The verified `/v1/stream-url` response contains one top-level signed URL and expiry metadata, not a `processedSources` or audio-choice list; do not invent source/audio controls. Continue to tolerate those optional fields if FrameX adds them, subject to host/expiry validation.

Preserve regular Series numeric season/episode selectors. Do not add an invented TV episode list; keep the range queue sequential and require the user's explicit action between browser handoffs. Never call a link handoff “download progress” or completion.

The current Anime integration uses the separate `/v1/anime/*` (Gotaku) route family. Keep AnimeX `/v1/animev2/*` as a distinct source family. A live probe verified search, 220 episode entries, and provider arrays shaped as `sub[]`/`dub[]` with `id`, `default`, and `tip`; add an episode-scoped provider selector and carry the selected provider through the server adapter only after input validation. Preserve source identity and server-side ownership of API credentials. AnimeX documents sub/dub availability but not caption-file tracks; do not claim unsupported subtitle behavior or downloads.

Extend the football Sports screen with a selected-match details view for documented detail, replay, rankings, head-to-head, stats, events, lineups, match-flow, has-new, and a lazy next-seven-days match list using verified Unix-second bounds. Add the Sports Resources route on demand with a truthful empty/error state. Load each match detail only when requested; preserve documented input names, show honest empty states, and strip all media/odds URLs from details. Add a separate Wrestling event/parts metadata mode and a NovelHub books/chapters reader using the live-verified route contracts; keep NovelStore groups unavailable after its 401 response. Keep Wrestling playback hidden because the verified stream probe returned HTTP 502 without establishing expiry or stable playback. Channels V2 playback is available only through the validated HLS relay or trusted YouTube embed paths, with no-store stream metadata and documented token refresh. Keep Showbox excluded, including records labeled Showbox in shared feeds. Treat all provider media availability as conditional; do not claim a stream works based only on catalog metadata.

A live FrameX `/v1/captions?subjectId=…` probe returned MovieBox SRT tracks from `cacdn.hakunaymatata.com`; a byte-limited check confirmed `application/x-subrip`, valid SRT timestamps, and `delay: 0` for the selected track. Add an on-demand same-origin captions route that resolves a signed MovieBox detail token, verifies the metadata subject/source, allows only HTTPS `.srt` files from that exact observed host, caps the file at 512 KiB, and converts SRT to WebVTT. Return only same-origin no-store track URLs and safe language labels to the browser; do not return or persist the upstream signed caption URLs. Show only zero-delay tracks until delay units are documented. Keep AnimeX caption-file controls out of the UI because its public route docs do not define them. Preserve support for `data.processedSources` and `data.processedSubtitles` if they appear in a future response, but the currently verified `/v1/stream-url` returns one top-level signed URL with expiry metadata and neither list. Do not fabricate source/audio choices.

Do not add a searchable music/MP3 section without a separate authorized and documented music API/provider. Do not integrate third-party URL-extraction routes from OmniSave.

### Device-local offline vault (no managed cloud database)

Keep the cloud database disabled. Store authorized offline media bytes only in this browser origin's OPFS, and store small title/job metadata in IndexedDB; never persist a FrameX key, signed media URL, or upstream caption URL. The current PWA uses a server-side, same-origin Range relay that resolves a signed title token and quality/episode, validates the upstream host and `206 Content-Range`, and streams bytes without buffering a complete movie or writing it to the server filesystem. Client writes are committed in bounded chunks so pause, retry, and resume can reconcile the real OPFS file size. Only mark an item completed after the file size matches the validated total. Allow local play/delete and explicit cleanup only for files created by this vault; never claim access to browser Downloads, arbitrary folders, or background transfers.

Feature-detect OPFS, IndexedDB, persistent-storage requests, and quota estimates; explain that a quota estimate is not a storage reservation or device free-space measurement. Refuse files whose total size or content type is not validated, handle quota/network/Range failures without reporting completion, and provide a cached offline shell through a service worker without caching media responses, signed URLs, or private API responses. The observed 4.12 GB one-byte Range sample is not proof of production multi-gigabyte relay limits; verify only small byte ranges locally and retain a truthful platform caveat until full transfer behavior is established.

### Speed and data handling

Use server-side caching for public catalog metadata, lazy/on-demand loading for optional categories and shelves, and concurrent fetches only for required records. Cache channel metadata and schedules briefly. Keep live sports freshness bounded and short. Keep the vault entirely device-local and cloud database disabled. Never cache short-lived media URLs or store the FrameX key in client code, metadata, logs, or public build output. Keep same-origin API handlers and source-neutral display copy.

Use static status text rather than spinners while requests are pending. The UI must never fabricate transfer progress, disk capacity, series episode counts, subtitle availability, or sports playback success. Preserve truthful browser download handoff wording and existing retry/error states.

## Project structure

- `app/layout.js`, `app/globals.css`, `app/loading.js`, `app/error.js`: global metadata, shell theme, static route-pending UI, and error recovery.
- `app/page.js`, `app/explore/page.js`: Discover and Explore server pages, streaming shell, search, category filters, and cached catalog data.
- `app/movie/[slug]/page.js`, `app/watch/[slug]/page.js`: title details and player route/query parsing.
- `app/shelf/page.js`, `app/downloads/page.js`, `app/profile/page.js`: device-local library, offline vault manager, and preferences.
- `app/api/home/route.js`, `app/api/search/route.js`, `app/api/stream/route.js`, `app/api/captions/route.js`, `app/api/download/route.js`, `app/api/anime/*`, and `app/api/sports/route.js`: existing same-origin handlers plus the verified subtitle proxy.
- `app/api/catalog/*`, `app/api/sports/*`, `app/api/channels/*`, `app/api/channels-v2/*`, `app/api/novels/*`, `app/api/wrestling/*`, and `app/api/offline/*`: same-origin, server-only adapters for verified families; media relays are bounded, Range-aware, and never arbitrary-URL proxies.
- `lib/catalog.js` and family normalizers: authenticated FrameX fetch, bounded metadata caches, source normalization, signed title tokens, canonical-ID validation, family separation, and media-link validation.
- `lib/offline-vault.js`, `components/OfflineVaultProvider.js`: device-local OPFS media files and IndexedDB metadata; no cloud media storage or signed-link persistence.
- `components/`: shared shell, Discover/Explore screens, NovelHub reader, Wrestling metadata, Channels guides, title/player views, Anime provider picker, Sports match details, local shelf actions, offline vault, and install controls.
- `public/manus-routes.json`: page-route manifest. New content-family views remain Explore modes; update this declaration only if a new page route is added.
- `public/beraflix-mark.svg`, `app.config.ts`: site/app mark and the required durable hosted platform logo metadata.
- `plan.md`, `TODO.md`, `.env.example`, `README.md`, `docs-upstream-notes.md`, `docs-framex-endpoint-audit.md`: approved approach, acceptance outcomes, safe setup, and accurate integration limitations.
- `Dockerfile`, `package.json`, `pnpm-lock.yaml`: keep the existing standalone deployment and pinned Next.js/React/pnpm toolchain.
